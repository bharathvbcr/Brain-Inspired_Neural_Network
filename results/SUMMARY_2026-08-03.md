### 1.1 Temporal information — order vs synchrony

`RESULT_2026-08-03_SHD_TEMPORAL_INFORMATION_H1.md` · 24 cells · 6 seeds ·
`published-2ms / adjacent-sum-5 / h512 / e100`

| manipulation | cost vs intact |
|---|---:|
| reverse time | 0.0032 |
| destroy order | 0.0189 |
| destroy order **and** synchrony | 0.1437 |
| **synchrony-specific increment** | **0.1248 — 6.6× the order effect** |

**H1 (rate coder): NOT SUPPORTED.** Mean difference 0.018919 passes the ≤0.02
equivalence bound; the 95% CIs are **disjoint by 0.0120** and the prereg
required both. The order effect is *below the registered bound for practical
negligibility* **and** *statistically reliable* — all six seeds positive.

**H3 (synchrony beyond order): SUPPORTED, confirmatory.** 0.1248, 6.2× its
bound. The prereg conditioned H3's confirmatory status on H1 failing; H1 failed,
so it activates as registered, with no post-hoc reclassification.

**H2: NOT RUN.** `rec+alif` at h512 produces zero usable cells.

**Caveat added 2026-08-04.** The 0.1248 synchrony increment is an **upper
bound**: `channel-shuffled` also moves the hidden layer's `saturated_fraction`
from exactly 0.0000 to 0.023–0.035 (all six seeds, inside the ≤0.05 gate) and
mean firing rate from 0.199 to 0.260. Some unquantified share of the increment
may be a regime effect rather than information loss. **H1 is unaffected** —
`bin-shuffled` saturates at exactly 0.0000 in every seed. See
`RESULT_2026-08-03_SHD_TEMPORAL_INFORMATION_H1.md` §6.7.

**Consequence for the matrix result.** `SHD_BPTT_CEILING_NEGATIVE_RESULT.md` §4
concluded "the forward is rate-coding" from resolution invariance. That inference
is now **withdrawn** — it was tested directly and refuted. The section has been
rewritten around **within-bin coincidence detection**, which explains resolution
invariance and order-sensitivity together.

## 2. Two things that could not be made to work

| attempt | outcome |
|---|---|
| **`rec+alif` at h512** | 0 of 3 seeds usable. Two abort on non-finite per-sample gradients (steps 220, 50); one completes with a gradient norm of 7.36e29. h128 and h256 are clean |
| **Gradient clipping** | 3 of 3 abort, and the previously-completing seed now fails at step 43. The abort fires *upstream* of where clipping acts — no threshold could work |

Three interventions have now failed on the h512 failure, each addressing the
symptom at the wrong stage: rescaling `W_rec` (the starting point), the f64
`l2_norm` fix (the record), batch clipping (the update step). The failure is in
the per-sample backward pass. Remaining candidates — truncated BPTT, lower
surrogate gain, spectral-radius-normalised init, narrower width — are model
changes needing registration.
