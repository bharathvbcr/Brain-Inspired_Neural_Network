Think of an excitable cell receiving little pushes. If several arrive close together, they can add up enough to trigger a spike.

Wait too long, and their effect fades. That is the intuition behind leaky integrate-and-fire.

What this result does—and does not—establish

Measured: the rate model loses more than three accuracy points in all 12 pairs. The attention advantage grows in all 12 comparisons, and by more than three points in 11/12. Both primary registered clauses are MET.

Design: deletion is fixed per example before training and used in both training and test sets. The twelve training seeds share one manipulation seed; these are not twelve independently sampled deletion-mask sets.

Open: why the advantage grows, whether it extends to other architectures, and whether an intact-trained model would survive sudden deletion at test time. There is no p70 condition here, so this page does not interpolate a dropout curve.

Learning: these models use backpropagation through time. This result does not establish that the original BINN local-learning rule succeeds.
